# Applied design system

This project applies the user-provided Hand-Drawn design system:

- Warm paper background: `#fdfbf7`
- Pencil foreground and border: `#2d2d2d`
- Correction marker red: `#ff4d4d`
- Ballpoint blue: `#2d5da1`
- Post-it yellow feature surfaces
- Kalam headings and Patrick Hand body
- Wobbly multi-value radii
- Hard offset shadows with no blur
- Tape, tack, dashed lines, scribbled arrows, and small rotations
- Mobile-first responsive gallery and touch targets
